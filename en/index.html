About

Profile

Sheet 01 / 05

Cartographer and geomatics engineer, I turn raw geospatial data into maps, tools and decisions. From field data collection to WebGIS deployment, I master the full chain: structuring geographic databases (PostgreSQL/PostGIS), satellite image processing, cartographic application development and process automation with Python and FME.

I combine technical rigour with field awareness - interactive cartography, remote sensing, spatial analysis and data science run through every project, with one constant goal: deliver reliable data and a map that speaks directly to decision-makers. ArcGIS, QGIS, Python, JavaScript, SQL, Google Earth Engine and FME are my everyday tools.

Trained in France, with experience across Côte d'Ivoire and France (local authorities, consulting firms, IGN, SUEZ), I enjoy tackling concrete geospatial challenges, independently or in a team, from project scoping through to end-user support.
